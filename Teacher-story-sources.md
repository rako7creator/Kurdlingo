# Teacher story sources

The 17 narratives and their characters are original fiction, written for language practice. Newroz's association with spring, Erbil's citadel above the city, and Hawraman's mountain settlement setting were checked against these primary sources. No source passages or traditional story texts are reproduced.

- UNESCO: https://www.unesco.org/en/days/nowruz
- UNESCO World Heritage: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1437/
- UNESCO World Heritage: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1647/

The broad continent outlines on the teaching globe/map are original schematic artwork, not a political boundary map. New model geometry, teacher scene and story text follow the game's original-code MIT license. Existing third-party model, font and synthesized-voice licenses still apply; see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.
